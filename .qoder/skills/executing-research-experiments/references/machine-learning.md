# Machine-Learning Execution Checklist

Use this checklist for implementing, training, resuming, and handing off research machine-learning models. A checked item means that the named evidence exists and is linked from the run manifest. A model name, successful process exit, or favorable metric alone is never acceptance evidence.

## Applicability and N/A

- Execute only checklist items applicable to the task contract and current stage.
- N/A is not a shortcut: record the exact contract/design basis and require reviewer agreement at handoff.
- Evidence that the selected model design or current stage is designed not to produce is legitimately N/A; evidence expected by the resolved contract but missing is a stop condition.
- Do not demand formal statistical repetition or final-test evidence during an implementation-only or development-smoke stage, but record downstream work as unaccepted and not assessed.
- Apply N/A only to evidence the resolved model design or stage does not require or produce. It cannot waive dataset identity, split identity, implementation validity, training provenance, or evaluation-lock evidence required for the current stage.

## Contract

- [ ] Record the immutable dataset version and content hash, split-manifest hash, schema version, and preprocessing or feature-pipeline hash consumed by the run.
- [ ] Define the input and output contract, including graph or tensor schema as applicable, field order, shape, dtype, units, masks, batching rules, supported domains, and target semantics.
- [ ] Define three operational input classes: supported ID inputs; a contract-declared OOD evaluation envelope outside training support that the model must accept for labeled, traceable evaluation; and unsupported or out-of-envelope inputs that the interface must reject.
- [ ] Record every OOD axis, training-support boundary, OOD-support or evaluation-envelope boundary, gap or separation rule, boundary ownership, and permitted cross-axis combination. Specify how inference output records its ID, OOD-axis, and combination labels.
- [ ] Record target normalization statistics, fitting partition, formula, units, and content hash. Implement and test the inverse transform used for reported predictions.
- [ ] Classify nodes, edges, conditions, independent variables, derived descriptors, targets, masks, and quality fields by causal and inference-time role. Prove that unavailable-at-inference or target-derived information cannot leak into inputs.
- [ ] Define the approved baseline set, model-capacity comparison, training budget, selection metric, and evaluation protocol before inspecting final-test results.
- [ ] Make capacity and budget comparisons explicit using relevant quantities such as trainable parameters, update count, data exposure, compute, runtime, or memory as required by the contract; document unavoidable mismatches.
- [ ] Define predeclared baseline competence gates, including reference capability and basic sanity checks. Permit reasonable architecture-specific tuning needed to reach those gates; do not weaken a baseline by forcing the candidate model's identical but unsuitable hyperparameters onto it.
- [ ] Record every baseline-specific search space, tuning decision, selected configuration, and resource difference. Explain how differences preserve budget fairness and apply the same validation-based selection discipline to baseline and candidate models.
- [ ] If a baseline fails a competence gate, use only one of these dispositions: fix and rerun it; replace it with a qualified baseline; withdraw or narrow the comparative claim; or label the comparison explicitly exploratory. Administrative approval alone cannot retain a confirmatory superiority claim based on an incompetent baseline.
- [ ] Before final-test access, freeze and content-hash the resolved configuration and analysis plan. The frozen selection registry must cover model and architecture, checkpoint, seed, retry policy, hyperparameters, preprocessing, normalization, loss, threshold, training budget, evaluation protocol, metric, aggregation unit and level, subgroup or slice, failure-exclusion rule, OOD axis, boundary, gap, cross-axis combination, baseline set and baseline tuning, statistical method, hypothesis or claim family, estimand, and outcome-contingent decision and reporting rule.
- [ ] State the scientific necessity claim for each model component and the comparison or ablation that can test it. Naming a model `Graph Transformer`, `Diffusion`, or another established architecture is not evidence that the component is necessary.
- [ ] Distinguish development smoke tests from formal statistical repetitions in the run manifest. Smoke tests validate wiring on limited data or steps; they cannot support scientific performance or uncertainty claims.
- [ ] Route confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, multiple-comparison control, aggregate uncertainty, and the required formal repetition design to the subsequent `evaluation-statistics` stage. Do not hard-code unapproved seed counts, thresholds, or statistical rules.

## Data Interface

- [ ] Validate every batch against the resolved schema before model execution, including required keys, field order, shapes, dtypes, devices, units, masks, finite values, and allowed domains.
- [ ] For graph models, verify graph boundaries, node and edge counts, edge-index bounds, node-to-graph assignments, condition broadcasting, mask semantics, and pooling inputs for single-graph and multi-graph batches.
- [ ] For PyTorch Geometric models only, test the applicable `Data` or `Batch` conventions, collation behavior, increment rules for index fields, `batch` and `ptr` consistency, dtype, and device movement. Do not require PyG evidence from non-PyG models.
- [ ] For dense, sequence, image, voxel, or other non-graph models, test their own contract-specific tensor and mask semantics rather than imposing graph or PyG requirements.
- [ ] For ordinary inductive evaluation, prove that normalization, vocabulary, imputation, feature-selection, augmentation, and other preprocessing state is fitted only on training data and is reused without refitting for validation and test.
- [ ] For a contract-authorized transductive or domain-adaptation evaluation, fit adaptation-specific preprocessing, normalization, or model state only from a separate adaptation set and the explicitly permitted unlabeled information. Freeze the adaptation algorithm, budget, stopping rule, selection rule, and configuration hash before access. The locked final evaluation set and its covariates must not participate in fitting, refitting, tuning, adaptation, selection, threshold choice, or any model or preprocessing-state update.
- [ ] Verify the target inverse transform with asymmetric anchors, boundary cases, and batch shapes. Compare restored units and values using the contract-approved policy.
- [ ] Route every valid input as supported ID or contract-declared OOD, preserve its axis and cross-axis labels through prediction and evaluation, and permit inference throughout the declared OOD envelope without silently treating it as invalid.
- [ ] Reject unknown method identifiers, missing required conditions, inputs outside both the supported ID domain and contract-declared OOD evaluation envelope, `NaN`, `Inf`, invalid shapes, and empty batches with explicit diagnostic errors before producing predictions.
- [ ] Preserve sample and causal-group identities through collation, shuffling, distributed sampling, prediction, and metric aggregation so every output traces back to the split manifest.

## Implementation Verification

- [ ] Run a minimal development smoke test that loads a real schema-conformant batch, executes the forward pass, computes the declared loss, runs backward, and confirms finite predictions, loss, and applicable gradients.
- [ ] Test batch sizes and graph or tensor sizes that expose broadcasting, singleton, padding, masking, and last-batch errors, without treating smoke coverage as formal statistical repetition.
- [ ] Verify required permutation or ordering invariance or equivariance with transformed copies of the same scientific object. Apply this gate only where the scientific contract requires that symmetry, and record the expected transformation of outputs.
- [ ] Save a checkpoint, load it into a fresh process or model instance, and compare parameters, buffers, preprocessing state, and deterministic-inference outputs under the contract-approved comparison policy.
- [ ] Test deterministic inference under a fully recorded inference configuration. If the contract intentionally uses stochastic inference, verify controlled replay from recorded state and characterize the intended variability instead of claiming determinism.
- [ ] Exercise successful inference, labeling, tracing, and metric routing for declared-OOD inputs at approved axis and cross-axis cases. Save identities, envelope decisions, predictions, and evaluation destinations.
- [ ] Exercise explicit rejection paths for invalid method identifiers, unsupported or out-of-envelope inputs, nonfinite inputs, malformed masks or indices, wrong dtype or device combinations, and empty batches. Save exception types and messages.
- [ ] Verify that metrics operate on inverse-transformed targets where the reporting contract requires physical units and that masking and aggregation preserve the intended sample-level denominator.
- [ ] Snapshot persistent model parameters and buffers, preprocessing state, adaptation state, and every other persistent state that could affect a later prediction immediately before and after a representative final-evaluation execution. Verify unchanged hashes or values to prove that evaluation performs no persistent or adaptive update.
- [ ] When the contract authorizes stochastic or diffusion inference, permit isolated local RNG or sampler state to advance during evaluation. Record the seed or initial RNG state, final RNG state where supported, sampler configuration and state needed for replay, and controlled-replay result; discard local state after the run rather than persisting it into later predictions.
- [ ] During locked final evaluation, do not feed sampled outputs back into the model or preprocessing state, threshold, retry policy, seed choice, candidate filtering or choice, or any other selection decision. Evaluate and retain candidates according to the pre-frozen protocol without outcome-driven cherry-picking.
- [ ] Run all applicable automated tests and archive command, environment, pass/fail/skip totals, duration, and logs.

## Training Discipline

- [ ] Record code commit, environment or lockfile hash, seed, fully resolved configuration and hash, device and accelerator details, precision mode, runtime, dataset and split hashes, and checkpoint hash for every run.
- [ ] Use validation data for every choice in the frozen selection registry. Seal final-test labels, predictions, target distributions, summaries, plots, and metrics until the registry, resolved-configuration hash, analysis-plan hash, model, checkpoint, protocol, and authorized final-evaluation command and command hash are frozen.
- [ ] During authorized final evaluation, permit only the content-hashed frozen final-evaluation command to read the locked final evaluation set and its covariates or labels. The command may perform only inference with no persistent or adaptive update and the pre-frozen metric computations and outcome-contingent reporting operations; contract-authorized isolated local RNG or sampler advancement is not a persistent update.
- [ ] Prevent label or target peeking: do not expose individual locked labels, targets, target-bearing intermediate values, predictions paired with labels, or unapproved summaries to model developers or decision makers outside the frozen evaluator and reporting rule.
- [ ] Record every final-test access event, including the authorized command's reads, with time, person or execution identity, purpose, command and command hash, input identities, resolved-configuration hash, analysis-plan hash, selection-registry hash, and hashes of labels, predictions, summaries, plots, metrics, and other produced artifacts.
- [ ] After any final-test label, prediction, target distribution, or metric is accessed, require a new, previously untouched locked test before changing or selecting any model or architecture, checkpoint, seed, retry policy, hyperparameter, preprocessing, normalization, loss, threshold, training budget, evaluation protocol, metric, aggregation unit or level, subgroup or slice, failure-exclusion rule, OOD axis, boundary, gap, cross-axis combination, baseline set or baseline tuning, analysis plan, statistical method, hypothesis or claim family, estimand, or outcome-contingent decision or reporting rule for confirmatory use.
- [ ] Apply the frozen outcome-contingent rule to report that a hypothesis or claim family is supported, refuted, or inconclusive. That faithful classification is not a post-test claim change; adding or reframing a hypothesis or claim family, changing its estimand, or changing the decision or reporting rule after access triggers the new-test-or-exploratory requirement.
- [ ] If no new, previously untouched locked test is available after such a post-access change or selection, downgrade every affected result and claim to explicitly exploratory and hand repeated-use analysis to `evaluation-statistics`. Neither a rationale nor administrative approval restores confirmatory status.
- [ ] Treat the authorized frozen-command read as a final-test access event subject to the same subsequent fresh-test-or-exploratory rule. The read itself is not a stop condition when the command performs no persistent or adaptive update, computes only pre-frozen metrics, records any permitted local RNG or sampler consumption, and produces the required ledger entry.
- [ ] Execute and report every seed declared by the approved run contract, including failed, interrupted, divergent, and excluded runs. Never select or report only the best seed.
- [ ] Define failure and exclusion rules before examining performance. Preserve logs, checkpoints or partial state, resource records, and failure classifications for every unsuccessful run.
- [ ] When resuming, restore and verify model, optimizer, scheduler, gradient-scaler where applicable, RNG, sampler or data-order state, epoch or update counters, and train-fitted preprocessing state.
- [ ] Compare an approved resumed-run anchor against uninterrupted behavior using the contract-defined determinism or tolerance policy. A checkpoint containing model weights alone is not evidence of exact training continuation.
- [ ] Record actual update counts, examples or causal groups seen, effective batch size, accumulation, learning-rate history, early-stopping decision, wall time, and peak resource use needed to audit the training budget.
- [ ] Prevent final-test information from driving any entry in the frozen selection registry, including architecture, hyperparameters, budget, protocol, metrics, aggregation, subgroup or slice definitions, failure exclusions, OOD definitions, baselines, statistical methods, hypothesis or claim families, estimands, and outcome-contingent decision or reporting rules. Enforce the fresh-test-or-exploratory rule from the first access event, not merely from an investigator's stated intent.

## Model-Specific Checks

- [ ] For a Graph Transformer, document graph construction, node and edge features, positional or structural encodings, attention masks, edge use, conditioning path, pooling or readout, and the causal role of every feature.
- [ ] For a Graph Transformer, test isolated nodes, variable graph sizes, batched graph boundaries, padding or attention masks, edge direction and duplication conventions, and pooling denominators.
- [ ] For a Graph Transformer, compare against contract-approved competent non-graph and simpler graph baselines under the resolved capacity, budget, selection, and evaluation protocol, allowing documented architecture-appropriate tuning within the fair resource envelope.
- [ ] For a Graph Transformer, run the ablations required to support claims about graph structure, edge information, attention, masking, conditioning, or pooling. Do not infer necessity from architecture naming.
- [ ] For a diffusion model, record prediction parameterization, forward-noise process, schedule and discretization, loss weighting, conditioning representation, condition-dropout policy, guidance method and scale, sampler, sampling steps, and stochastic-state handling.
- [ ] For a diffusion model, verify schedule endpoints and indexing, forward-noise and target construction, parameterization conversions, condition alignment, and finite training loss using analytically checkable or contract-approved anchors.
- [ ] For a diffusion model, verify sampling shape, dtype, device, conditioning, guidance, reproducibility mode, and output-domain constraints across representative batch sizes and supported methods.
- [ ] For a diffusion model, preserve every generated candidate's seed or RNG state, sampler configuration, checkpoint hash, condition identity, raw output, validity decision, and selection status.
- [ ] When multiple diffusion samples support a diversity claim, retain the full candidate set and calculate the contract-defined diversity measures; do not present cherry-picked examples as diversity evidence.
- [ ] When generated candidates are forwarded to simulation or reranked, preserve candidate-to-forward-job provenance, all failures and exclusions, the reranking rule and code revision, scores available at selection time, and the final selection mapping.
- [ ] Treat Graph Transformer and diffusion checks as conditional on the resolved architecture. Record the exact design basis for N/A rather than forcing every model through every model-specific check.

## Evaluation Handoff

- [ ] Provide the fully resolved training and inference configurations with hashes, environment or lockfile, code commit, dataset and split hashes, and exact working-directory-aware commands.
- [ ] Provide complete logs and run manifests for every declared seed and failed run, including device, runtime, resource use, selection status, and failure or exclusion disposition.
- [ ] Provide checkpoint files and hashes, checkpoint-selection evidence, save-load verification, deterministic-inference or controlled-stochasticity evidence, and resume-state coverage.
- [ ] Provide baseline definitions, competence-gate results, tuning records, and results under the approved capacity, budget, preprocessing, split, selection, and evaluation protocol. If a gate failed, document only the permitted fix/rerun, qualified replacement, claim withdrawal or narrowing, or exploratory disposition.
- [ ] Provide schema and data-interface validation, target normalization and inverse-transform evidence, finite forward/backward smoke results, rejection-path tests, and applicable invariance evidence.
- [ ] Provide model-specific configuration and verification evidence, including graph/edge/mask/pooling and ablation records or diffusion schedule/conditioning/guidance/sampling/diversity/forward-rerank provenance as applicable.
- [ ] Provide the frozen evaluation command and command hash, checkpoint identity, seed list, architecture and hyperparameter configuration, training budget, evaluation protocol, metric configuration, inverse-transform configuration, aggregation unit and level, subgroup and slice rules, failure exclusions, OOD axes and envelope rules, baseline definitions and tuning, statistical method, hypothesis or claim families, estimands, outcome-contingent decision and reporting rules, resolved-configuration hash, analysis-plan hash, selection-registry hash, persistent-state no-update verification, permitted local RNG or sampler initial/final state and controlled-replay evidence, and authorized test-access ledger with times, actors, purposes, reads, and artifact hashes needed by the downstream evaluator.
- [ ] For transductive or domain-adaptation evaluation, provide adaptation-set identities, the permitted unlabeled-information schema, frozen adaptation algorithm, budget, stopping and selection rules, configuration hash, and proof that the locked final evaluation set did not participate in adaptation.
- [ ] State whether the final-test evidence remains confirmatory, was moved to a new, previously untouched locked test after post-access changes, or was downgraded to exploratory with repeated-use analysis routed to `evaluation-statistics`.
- [ ] Label development smoke results separately from formal runs and hand off unresolved statistical design to `evaluation-statistics`; do not translate smoke repetitions into formal sample sizes or performance claims.

## Stop Conditions

Stop training, resumption, evaluation, or handoff as appropriate; preserve diagnostics and mark the run unresolved when any of the following occurs:

- [ ] Dataset version or hash, split-manifest hash, schema, target normalization, preprocessing state, or checkpoint identity required for the current stage is missing or drifts.
- [ ] Node, edge, condition, independent-variable, derived-descriptor, target, mask, or quality-field roles are ambiguous, or unavailable-at-inference or target-derived data leak into inputs.
- [ ] Batch shape, dtype, device, mask, graph-boundary, target inverse transform, or identity tracing violates the resolved interface contract.
- [ ] A forward or backward smoke test produces nonfinite predictions, loss, or required gradients, or applicable permutation/order behavior violates the scientific contract.
- [ ] Checkpoint save-load equivalence, deterministic inference, or contract-approved controlled stochastic replay cannot be reproduced.
- [ ] Supported ID, contract-declared OOD, and unsupported input boundaries are missing or ambiguous; OOD axes, training support, OOD evaluation envelopes, gap or separation rules, or cross-axis combinations are unresolved; or declared-OOD inference, labeling, tracing, and evaluation fail.
- [ ] Unknown methods, unsupported or out-of-envelope inputs, `NaN`, `Inf`, malformed inputs, or empty batches are accepted silently.
- [ ] After final-test labels, predictions, target distributions, or metrics are accessed, any model or architecture, checkpoint, seed, retry policy, hyperparameter, preprocessing, normalization, loss, threshold, training budget, evaluation protocol, metric, aggregation unit or level, subgroup or slice, failure-exclusion rule, OOD axis, boundary, gap, cross-axis combination, baseline set or baseline tuning, analysis plan, statistical method, hypothesis or claim family, estimand, or outcome-contingent decision or reporting rule changes or is selected without moving confirmatory evaluation to a new, previously untouched locked test or downgrading every affected result and claim to exploratory for `evaluation-statistics` handling. Reporting supported, refuted, or inconclusive exactly as the frozen rule requires is not a stop condition.
- [ ] A final-test access event, including an authorized evaluation read, lacks its time, person or execution identity, purpose, command and command hash, input identities, resolved-configuration hash, analysis-plan hash, selection-registry hash, or produced-artifact hashes; a run reports only the best seed, omits a declared seed or failed run, or changes failure rules after observing performance.
- [ ] A resumed run lacks optimizer, scheduler, applicable scaler, RNG, data-order, counter, or preprocessing state required by the resume contract.
- [ ] A baseline misses its predeclared reference-capability or sanity gate and is not fixed and rerun, replaced by a qualified baseline, removed through withdrawal or narrowing of the comparative claim, or labeled explicitly exploratory; administrative approval alone is not a valid remedy for a confirmatory superiority claim.
- [ ] A baseline is weakened by unsuitable forced hyperparameters or lacks architecture-appropriate tuning, selection, capacity, budget-fairness, or evaluation-protocol evidence required for the scientific comparison.
- [ ] An ordinary inductive preprocessing state is fitted outside training data, or a transductive or domain-adaptation state is fitted outside the independent adaptation set and permitted unlabeled information or uses an unfrozen adaptation algorithm, budget, stopping rule, selection rule, or configuration.
- [ ] The locked final evaluation set or its covariates participate in fitting, refitting, tuning, adaptation, selection, threshold choice, or any model or preprocessing-state update.
- [ ] The locked final evaluation set, its covariates, or its labels are read outside the content-hashed frozen final-evaluation command; that command performs non-frozen metric computation or a persistent or adaptive state update; or individual labels, targets, target-bearing intermediates, or unapproved summaries are exposed for peeking. Contract-authorized isolated local RNG or sampler advancement is not a stop condition when it is recorded and replayable.
- [ ] During locked final evaluation, sampled outputs feed back into model or preprocessing state, threshold, retry policy, seed choice, candidate filtering or choice, or any other selection decision.
- [ ] A graph-model claim lacks required graph, edge, mask, pooling, non-graph baseline, or ablation evidence, or a diffusion claim lacks required parameterization, schedule, conditioning, guidance, sampling, diversity, or forward/rerank provenance.
- [ ] Architecture naming is used in place of evidence that a model component is scientifically necessary.
- [ ] The handoff lacks the resolved configuration, environment, complete logs, checkpoint hash, declared-seed and failed-run records, baseline evidence, or exact evaluation command required by the current stage.
- [ ] Formal statistical conclusions are drawn from development smoke tests, or unapproved seed counts, thresholds, or statistical rules are introduced instead of routing the design to `evaluation-statistics`.
- [ ] Evidence required by the resolved contract for the current stage is missing. Design-inapplicable evidence may be N/A only with its exact contract/design basis and handoff-reviewer agreement.
